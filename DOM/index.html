Spider Man

About

Spider-Man is a fictional superhero that appears in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko, the character first appeared in the anthology comic book Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962).

Creation & Development

The character was created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko, and first appeared in the anthology comic book Amazing Fantasy #15 (August 1962). He Said the idea for the character came to him while he was working on a story about a young man with spider-like abilities. As with Fantastic Four, Lee saw Spider-Man as a way to explore themes of responsibility and the consequences of one's actions.
